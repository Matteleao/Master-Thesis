A
Purpose and Use

This review aggregates follow-up performance for one study, one site and one defined period. It determines whether required follow-up was obtained within the applicable window and with the assessments needed for endpoint and safety interpretation.

Use linked PFVRs and source records; do not copy participant narratives or create a parallel exception file.

Unit of analysisDecision supportInputsOutputs
Study–site–period; participants due, expected visits and required components.Whether follow-up remains controlled, needs monitoring, requires corrective action or must be escalated.PFVRs, CIP schedule, component rules, outreach evidence and ICQL when imaging is required.A quantitative result and decision routed to PIR FC-01, IMC, CAPA, SRR, PI oversight or the applicable record.
