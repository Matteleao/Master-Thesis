A
Case opening, scope and immediate factual record

Purpose: Open one SART for one occurrence, observed device deficiency or new safety information. The record follows the case from initial awareness to controlled closure and preserves the separate assessment of adverse event, device deficiency, seriousness, expectedness and relationships.

Boundary: This is a site-level controlled record. It does not replace source records, the CRF, sponsor safety forms, device accountability, a deviation record, SCML, CSST, CAPA or regulatory submissions. It links to them only where relevant.

ElementComplete whenRecord / interface
Unit of analysisOne SART is opened for one occurrence, deficiency or material new safety information.Coded participant, SART ID and controlled case location are linked.
Decision supportedThe case is triaged, protected, communicated, followed and closed using applicable controlled procedures.Current status, classification, communications and closure decision.
InputsFactual information from source, participant contact, procedure, device accountability, imaging or other authorized source.Reference IDs or locations; do not reproduce full clinical narratives.
OutputsIndividual safety record, case communications and structured signals for SCML/CSST, CAPA, SRR or PIR when applicable.Recipient, reference, owner and next action.
