A
Purpose and use

The FUCR reads follow-up across all participants and visits, using the Participant Follow-up Visit Records (PFVR) without reopening them. It gives the four follow-up measures against the rule (B), maps every visit to date (C), shows which components fail at which time point (D) and how far visits drift from target (E), and looks for where problems concentrate and why (F). Its result feeds indicator 3 of the Performance Indicator Record (PIR).
