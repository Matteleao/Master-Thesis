A
Purpose and Use
Account for every coded person considered for this investigation, their current screening status and final outcome. Reconcile eligibility and enrollment references at the cut-off; the PSEAR holds the individual eligibility decision.
ElementScope / DecisionRecord / Link
Unit of analysisOne coded person in one log row, updated as the pathway changes.Candidate code and sheet/line reference; no direct identifiers.
Decision supportedCan the site account for every candidate and resolve missing or conflicting links?Status/outcome, PSEAR and enrollment references; reconciliation issue ID.
InputsAuthorized identification source, current CIP, PSEAR and enrollment records.Controlled record IDs/locations; keep full evidence in the source.
OutputsPopulation counts, primary non-inclusion categories and reconciliation status.PIR; IMC, POT, SRR, CAPA or sponsor only when a material/recurring issue requires routing.
B
Coding, Confidentiality and Use Rules
Use candidate codes only; do not enter candidate names, personal initials, birth dates, contact details or clinical narratives. Keep the identity key separately under controlled access. Update the same row; preserve changes through the controlled record process. Cite PSEAR/source records instead of copying them. A pre-screen-only candidate does not require a PSEAR before formal assessment exists.
