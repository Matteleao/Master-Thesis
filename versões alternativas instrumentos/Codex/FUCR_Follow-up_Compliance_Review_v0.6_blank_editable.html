A
Purpose and Use
Review follow-up across one site, a defined population and a fixed period. Aggregate reconciled PFVR records to assess timing, component completeness, endpoint-critical omissions and recovery of participants due for follow-up.
ElementScope / DecisionRecord / Link
Unit of analysisOne site, follow-up population, review period and data cut-off.FUCR ID; visit schedule; source population and controlled extract.
Decision supportedIs the follow-up process controlled, under observation or requiring action?Four core measures; approved criteria; limitations and disposition.
InputsPFVR visit/component states, schedule rules, recovery evidence and relevant source summaries.PFVR IDs/ranges and extract version; IQL or other specialized outputs as applicable.
OutputsAggregate results, trends, causes, accountable actions and receiving records.PIR, IMC, SRR, CAPA, POT or sponsor as applicable. Individual clinical and safety assessments remain in their source records.

Apply the rules effective for this period. Define components, endpoint criticality, status criteria, exclusions and comparison rules before reviewing results. Attendance, component completeness and component timing are separate states.
