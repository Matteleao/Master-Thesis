A
Purpose and Use

Purpose: Document one coded participant’s eligibility decision before enrollment, with the evidence and required reviews that support it.

Sources: Reference the current CIP, consent source, clinical records, IQL/imaging reports, SIFSL and CRF/eCRF or sponsor records. Record only the details needed to reconstruct the decision; retain full narratives in their controlled sources.
