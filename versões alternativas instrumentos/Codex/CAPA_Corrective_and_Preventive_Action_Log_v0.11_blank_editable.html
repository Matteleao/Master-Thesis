A
Purpose and Use — CAPA portfolio log
Use one controlled site-level portfolio review for the stated period, cut-off and source population. Register each authorized QMS CAPA once, including new CAPAs, active carry-forward and reopening events under the inclusion rule. Keep CAPAs closed during the period. This log reconciles and reviews the portfolio; the authorized individual CAPA retains its investigation, action plan and closure authority.
ElementComplete whenRecord / link
Unit of analysisOne row in the Master CAPA Register represents one QMS CAPA. The review evaluates the complete CAPA portfolio for the defined site and period.CAPA ID; source/QMS reference; CTQ or operational domain; category; criticality; lifecycle status.
Decision supportThe reviewer can decide whether the CAPA portfolio is reconciled and controlled, whether a pattern needs escalation, and which active CAPAs require follow-up or closure review.Reconciliation result; distribution; signal assessment; active follow-up; effectiveness/closure decision.
InputsComplete from the authorized CAPA/QMS register and relevant linked records. Use IDs and controlled locations rather than copying case narratives.QMS CAPA; deviation/investigation; SART; IMC; FUCR; IQL; SCML; PIR; SRR; controlled SOP/CIP records.
OutputsThe review creates a reconstructable portfolio status and routes any required update to the accountable record or reviewer.CAPA follow-up index; escalation; SRR/PIR/IMC/process update; controlled handoff; PI decision.

Assign one primary Category, one consequence-based Criticality and the authorized-source Lifecycle status. Keep CtQ/domain separate. CAT-10/local mapping or CAT-P requires rationale and review; pending classification is an unresolved limitation.

CriticalityOperational definitionMinimum review consequence
HighActual or potential effect on participant rights/safety, critical endpoint integrity, material compliance, device accountability, or serious/continuous non-conformance.PI review and applicable escalation route; next review date required.
MediumRelevant effect on quality, execution or reconstructability without a current High-criticality condition.Owner and next milestone required; review in the next CAPA cycle.
LowLocalized and contained issue without material current CTQ impact and without a systemic pattern.Routine follow-up; reclassify if recurrence, extent or evidence changes.
Lifecycle statusMeaningRequired use in this log
Open — analysis/plan pendingCause, authorized plan or required route is not yet complete.Continue in Section F.
Implementation in progressAn authorized plan exists; one or more actions are not completely implemented.Continue in Section F.
Effectiveness pendingActions are implemented; a defined population, period or evidence is still needed to judge effectiveness.Continue in F and evaluate in G.
Closed — effectiveThe effectiveness criterion is met and closure is documented in the authorized record.Include in D; use G for closure confirmation if reviewed this period.
Closed — documented limitationClosure is authorized with a stated limit to effectiveness judgment and accepted residual risk.Use G; cite the authorized decision, limitation and acceptance.
ReopenedRecurrence, ineffective action or material new information requires the prior CAPA to reopen.Escalate and return to F.
Important boundary: Criticality is a management priority for the CAPA portfolio. It is not a substitute for adverse-event seriousness, device-deficiency classification or any safety decision. Use the applicable QMS, CIP and sponsor rule where a local rule differs.

Master CAPA Register — one row per CAPA opened
One row per authorized CAPA ID. Inclusion basis: New (opened in period), Carry (active at start), Reopened (reopened in period), or History (context only). Keep History separate from current distributions. Record opened/closed/reopened dates when applicable. Keep the problem factual; identify original source type and owner in the linked CAPA/source reference.
