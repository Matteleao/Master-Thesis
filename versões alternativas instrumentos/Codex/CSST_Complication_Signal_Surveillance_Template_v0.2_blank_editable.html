A
Purpose and Use
Use one CSST for a defined procedure/device population and review period, or a governed ad hoc trigger. Report reconciled SCML snapshots and reproducible analytical outputs; assess patterns and select the response. Individual case assessment remains in SART — Safety Assessment, Reporting and Tracking Template — and the applicable sponsor/CEC records.
