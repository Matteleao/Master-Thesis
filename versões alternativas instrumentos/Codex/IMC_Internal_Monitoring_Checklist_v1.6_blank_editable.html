A
Purpose and Use of This Checklist

Purpose and Use: Execute one IMP-defined review cycle for one site, period or trigger. Assess control execution, reconstructable evidence and effectiveness/response separately. Reference specialized sources, SRR, POT, safety, deviation and CAPA records; retain their assessments in the owning record.

Use the approved scope, sampling rule and IMP version. Document prospective changes or PI decisions; do not redefine the rules after observing results. Every negative or uncertain classification needs a finding, limitation, response or linked record.

B
Monitoring Classification Guide
Assess Execution, Evidence and Effectiveness / Response separately: an activity can be complete and evidenced yet reveal an ineffective control.

Execution records whether the planned activity occurred, not whether its result was satisfactory.

Execution
ClassificationGeneral definitionUse when
CompleteThe monitoring activities applicable to the domain were performed during the review period.All planned activities for the period were completed, even if they identified a signal requiring action.
PartialSome relevant monitoring activities were performed, but one or more material elements were incomplete.Part of the planned review occurred, or the review covered only a limited subset without an approved scope limitation.
Not performedThe due control activity did not occur or was not completed.Document non-execution and recovery. Missing records alone are an evidence limitation; establish what occurred.
Not applicableThe activity was not applicable to the stated study phase, review period, or selected scope.A documented scope decision explains why no activity was expected.

Classification Guide
B
Monitoring Classification Guide — continued

Evidence confirms what occurred and what was found. Effectiveness considers whether the control and response worked.

Evidence
ClassificationGeneral definitionUse when
AdequateEvidence is sufficient, traceable, and allows the reviewer to confirm execution and outcome.The linked controlled record or combination of records is available and internally consistent.
PartialEvidence exists but is incomplete, dispersed, delayed, or does not confirm every relevant element.The reviewer can confirm part of the activity but needs clarification or additional records.
Insufficient / unavailableEvidence cannot confirm that the activity occurred or what result it produced.Records are missing, contradictory, illegible, or not retrievable within the review.
Not applicableNo specific evidence was expected for the documented scope.Use only when the corresponding execution item is also not applicable.
Effectiveness / Response
ClassificationGeneral definitionRequired response
EffectiveThe control is functioning as intended and no unresolved relevant signal remains.No action is required unless another dimension or linked review requires it.
ObservationA minor issue or improvement point exists without current evidence of material control failure.Record the observation; add an action if follow-up is needed.
Not effectiveA recurrence, trend, significant gap, ineffective response, or unresolved relevant signal was identified.Signal, action, owner, due date, and escalation or linked record are mandatory.
Not assessableEffectiveness cannot be determined from the available information.Document the limitation and create an action to complete the assessment.
Review result logic
Execution / Evidence / EffectivenessInterpretationMinimum Response
Complete / Adequate / EffectiveControl occurred, is reconstructable and has no unresolved relevant signal.Record the result; retain linked review references.
Complete / Adequate / Not effectiveExecution and evidence are sound; the control or response failed.Record finding, owner, due date and action/escalation.
Complete / Partial / Not assessableRecords support execution but not the full effectiveness assessment.Record the limitation and evidence/follow-up action.
Complete / Insufficient / Not assessableExecution is established elsewhere; reviewed evidence cannot support effectiveness.Link the basis for execution and recover/qualify evidence.
Not performed / AdequateEvidence establishes that the due activity did not occur.Adequate evidence does not mean execution occurred; recover and reassess.
Partial / Any evidenceDue activity remains incomplete.Record recovery, owner, due date and applicable route.
Not applicableApproved scope explains why no activity was expected.Cite the exclusion/rationale; do not use N/A for missing evidence.

07 · Endpoint data agreementA predefined sample of endpoint-critical fields first recorded outside CRF was compared with the authoritative source.Sample/rule definition; source and CRF/database entries; comparison sheet, queries/corrections and discrepancy log; ICDL, PFVR, IQL or SART as applicable.Entries agree with source, discrepancies are corrected/explained and unresolved disagreement has a linked action or limitation.
08 · Timeliness, backlog and closure disciplineNotes, data entry, queries, actions and internal reviews due in the period were checked.Query log, action log, IMC findings, CAPA/PIR, completion timestamps.Backlog and overdue work are visible, owned and controlled.
09 · External context reviewRelevant literature, registry findings, safety alerts and field notices were considered when applicable.Approved external review record, field notice, sponsor communication, PI note.Relevant external information is interpreted against local findings and escalated when needed.
10 · Early complication pattern reviewEarly complications were reviewed for timing, severity, intervention, surgeon, case type and device/procedure context.SCML, CSST, SART, SLCT, PFVR and imaging records.Patterns are either reasonably explained or escalated without replacing individual event adjudication.
