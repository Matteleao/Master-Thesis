A
Scope, decision and interfaces

Purpose and use: Complete this controlled record during the procedure, not retrospectively. It captures critical procedural execution, unit-level device traceability, intraoperative decisions and immediate post-procedure review at the point of generation. It supports, but does not replace, the operative source note, authoritative device-accountability record, safety reporting, deviation documentation, imaging source records or CAPA.

Device traceability and reconciliation
E
Device Accountability and Component-Level Traceability

Record every component used, including auxiliary, replacement, trial, explanted or discarded components where applicable. Copy identifiers from the label or authorised source at the time of use. Do not infer missing identifiers later. Reconcile the final configuration against the operative note and the authoritative device-accountability record before case closure. If a component or final configuration cannot be reconciled, do not close the case as complete; record the linked route in Section F.
