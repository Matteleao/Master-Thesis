A
Purpose and Use
Review six predefined site-performance indicators for one study, site and period. Decide whether each is within its control rule, needs closer review, requires action or cannot be assessed. Reuse controlled source records and retain their decisions and ownership.
ElementScope / DecisionRecord / Link
Unit of analysisOne indicator ID, study–site–period and defined population or activity base.Period, cut-off, rule version and exact source boundary.
Decision supportedWithin control; Closer review; Action required; Not assessable.E: rule comparison and bounded interpretation; G: response.
InputsProspective rules, reconciled source records and comparable prior periods.B–D: scope, configuration and source basis; F: measures.
OutputsReproducible results, named receiving routes, follow-up and period conclusion.G: actions; H: verification; I: accountable review.
B
Review Population, Period and Scope
Define the actual review boundary before calculating results. Use counts with their units; a percentage alone can hide small denominators or important events. Keep incompatible activity bases separate.
Review ItemBoundary / DefinitionExact Reference / Limitation
Population / activity basesSpecify inclusions, procedures, expected visits, reportable cases and critical-data units.
Source coverage / exclusionsIdentify records, date ranges, excluded or late records and effects on the denominator.
Rule or source changesState none, or identify versions/effective dates and split the period where required.
Prior-period comparabilityConfirm common definitions/rules or explain how changes limit the comparison.
Scope Limitations / Review Reason / Comparability Decision
Fix definitions, scope, sources, cadence, thresholds and routes before first enrollment. Amend rules prospectively under a new version/effective date. An unresolved source gap may require a Not assessable result and a recovery route.

G
Signal, Decision and Action Route
Record findings that require a response. Routes: Monitoring; Closer review; Correction; CAPA; Escalation; Evidence recovery for Not assessable. State the specific receiving record/section and named owner. Recurrence, systemic failure and materiality inform CAPA or escalation under the configured rule.
Signal / Indicator IDFinding / Impact / MaterialityDecision / RouteReceiving Record / Section / Action IDNamed OwnerDue DateAction State / Next Review

The receiving record owns investigation and action closure. Urgent safety or authorization concerns follow their immediate route; do not wait for the next PIR. Continue this register on numbered sheets.

H
Action Verification and Effectiveness
Carry current and prior PIR actions forward. Define the acceptance criterion when assigning the response; later record evidence and the effectiveness result separately from completion. A completed action without verification remains identifiable as pending.
Action / Signal IDAction Type / Receiving RecordEffectiveness Criterion / Defined CheckEvidence / Comparable Period / Check DateEffectiveness ResultClosure / Continued Route / Owner

Effectiveness: Pending; Demonstrated; Not demonstrated; Not yet assessable with limitation. Record whether the action remains open or closes under its owning record, with basis and follow-up. A documented limitation must not be relabeled Effective.
