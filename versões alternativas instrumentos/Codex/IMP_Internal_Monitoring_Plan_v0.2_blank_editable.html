Purpose and Use
Define how this site will verify its critical controls before enrollment begins and throughout the investigation. Set the scope, cadence, sample, reviewers, criteria and response routes prospectively. The IMC records each review performed under this plan.
ElementScope / DecisionRecord / Link
Unit of analysisOne site, one investigation and one controlled monitoring plan for a stated validity period.Plan ID/version, effective date, approval and controlled location.
Decision supportedAre internal verification arrangements proportionate, executable and authorized before use?B: boundary; C–F: check configuration; G: response; H: implementation/approval.
InputsCurrent CIP and manuals, site risks, authorized roles, training and controlled source records.Identify the applicable versions and source map in B; specify each check in C–F.
OutputsAn approved review schedule, sample/evidence plan, decision criteria and escalation route.The IMC cites this version. Findings, clinical assessments and individual CAPA remain in their owning records.

G
Response and Escalation Routes
Configure the owner, trigger and follow-up for each finding state. Classify execution, evidence and effectiveness separately. An isolated corrected issue can remain in IMC with evidence; recurrence, material impact or failed response may require a governed route. Do not delay an applicable safety notification while completing an IMC review.

Local Routing Rules / Contact and Governing Procedure Reference
PFVR/FUCR: follow-up; IQL: image-set quality; ICDL: procedure/device source; PIR: performance interpretation; SRR: risk; POT/MRL: accountable decisions. Identify applicable timelines from the controlled CIP/procedure.

IMC ID/date, IMP version/check, factual finding/evidence, impact, initial response, reason for CAPA referral, linked records and receiving owner/trigger. Keep causal investigation and the detailed plan in the individual QMS CAPA.
