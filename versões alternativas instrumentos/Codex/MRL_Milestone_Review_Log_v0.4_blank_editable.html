A
Purpose and Use
Record the PI’s formal decision to permit, conditionally permit or block a defined next step at a configured milestone. Base the decision on an observable trigger, a defined implementation unit and retrievable evidence. Calendar reviews and urgent cases follow their own records.
ElementScope / DecisionRecord / Link
Unit of analysisOne gate decision for a defined site, surgeon, process, cohort or close-out unit.Decision Entry ID; milestone/configuration version; unit and denominator.
Decision supportedMay this unit take the requested next step, within what scope and restrictions?G: PI decision; H: conditions; J: clearance or re-review.
InputsProspectively configured trigger, denominator, gate standard and relevant specialist evidence.C: configuration; E: source package; F: sufficiency for this gate.
OutputsPermitted/prohibited scope, accountable condition, clearance evidence and next gate or re-review trigger.D: decision index; receiving record and controlled filing in K.
B
Decision Model and Record Boundaries
Configure the gate before the relevant evidence accrues. Assemble the applicable sources, assess sufficiency, then record the PI decision. Changes to triggers, scope or standards apply prospectively under a new approved version.
RecordFunctionMRL Interface
MRLFormal permission, bounded permission or block at a configured gate.Record the next step and exact unit affected; other units may remain in different phases.
POTActive PI oversight, discussion, escalation and follow-up during conduct.Reference the relevant oversight decision; retain meeting/action detail in POT.
IMP / IMCPlan and execute internal verification of operational controls.Use their relevant results; assess sufficiency here without repeating the review.
Specialist / QMS recordsClinical, safety, qualification, risk, performance and authorized CAPA decisions.Cite their current status. An MRL decision or cleared condition does not close those records.
Participants progress asynchronously. A gate governs the specified implementation unit and organizational transition. Urgent safety, device or authorization concerns require their applicable route immediately; do not wait for a milestone.

M0 records local capability only; sponsor selection and institutional authorization retain their own routes. M1 addresses executable readiness, not form completion. M3 does not certify a completed learning curve (SLCT). For M4 specify immediate postoperative, first follow-up, retention/completeness or a justified combination. Milestone Decision Index
Index each completed, signed gate decision once. Retain the detailed E–K record for that entry. Record scope and current condition/block status; a later re-review links back to the previous decision.

Decision Standard
DecisionUse WhenRequired Scope / Response
PassTrigger, unit and denominator are correct; relevant evidence supports the specified next step and known limitations do not prevent it.State the permitted scope. Pass is not a conclusion that clinical outcomes are favorable.
Pass with ConditionsEvidence supports a bounded next step under an explicit restriction.State permitted/prohibited scope, owner/destination, clearance evidence, objective trigger and consequence if unmet.
BlockedA trigger, essential control, authorization or decision-critical evidence is absent, insufficient or materially uncertain.State exactly what cannot proceed and the evidence/re-review needed. The block need not apply to the entire study.
