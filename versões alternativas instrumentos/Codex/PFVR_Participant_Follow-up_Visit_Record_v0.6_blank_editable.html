A
Purpose and Use
Document one participant’s scheduled follow-up visit: attendance and timing, required components, relevant findings, exceptions and final use. Visit timing and component timing are separate. FUCR aggregates these records across participants and visits.
ElementScope / DecisionRecord / Link
Unit of analysisOne participant, one nominal visit and one linked visit record.Participant, visit and PFVR IDs; source/schedule version.
Decision supportedWas the visit performed as required, and for what intended assessment is it usable?C: visit timing; D/E: component evidence; I–K: synthesis and outcome.
InputsApplicable visit schedule/methods, component evidence and clinical/source records.Enter the required visit components once in D.
OutputsTraceable component results, visit classification, cause/impact and accountable follow-up.FUCR uses visit/component counts; J owns the local response route.
B
Applicable Visit Component Guide
The current CIP/visit schedule defines the actual components in D. These families are reminders, not a universal checklist. N/A requires a source basis.
Imaging / objective tests: D: execution and method; E: intended-use review / IQL reference.
Required PROMs: D: each required instrument; F: relevant participant-reported change.
Clinical / functional assessment: D: required execution; G: concise finding and clinical-source reference.
Care / safety review: Link the clinical/safety source; J: response requiring follow-up.
C
