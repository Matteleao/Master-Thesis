Classify Lessons and Their Destinations
Lesson Type Guide
Retain: Keep an evidenced useful practice.
Change: Revise an existing practice or control.
Add: Introduce a missing practice or control.
Retire: Stop an obsolete or unhelpful practice.
Escalate: Seek a decision beyond this debrief.
Limitation: Preserve uncertainty or a bounded constraint.
A lesson states a specific practice to retain or change, with supporting evidence and a destination. Record its scope in F. Evidence limit: None identified; Bounded; Unresolved, with the G or receiving-record reference.

Priority: High: safety, endpoint, traceability or recurrent consequential failure. Medium: control improvement with plausible recurrence. Low: useful refinement without a consequential gap. Priority guides transfer; it does not reclassify source events.

Transfer Lessons into Controlled Change
Carry the Lesson ID into each receiving record. Scope: standing site practice; future study; ongoing study; sponsor clarification; no transfer, with basis. The receiving record owns its action, implementation and closure. Reference an existing CAPA when it already owns the response.

Transfer stages: Planned; Accepted by receiver; Transferred; Implemented; No transfer justified. H records evidence and effectiveness separately. A handoff or document release alone does not demonstrate effectiveness.

Reprint this page when needed, retaining the PIDLR and Lesson IDs. Record the exact receiving reference and owner before closing a debrief with tracked transfer items.

H
Verify Transfer and Separate Effectiveness
Transferred requires documented handoff or release. Implemented requires evidence of use. Effective requires completion of the predefined verification. Future effectiveness may remain pending in a controlled receiving record after the debrief closes.
