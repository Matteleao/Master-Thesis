A
Purpose, scope and record boundaries

Purpose: Maintain a controlled record of persons considered for the clinical investigation, their coded identification, administrative outcome and reason for non-inclusion or withdrawal. Use one line per subject or screening candidate, according to the approved study process.

Boundary: This log does not decide eligibility and does not replace the participant-level PSEAR, informed-consent record, source documents, CRF, safety record or sponsor files. It records the outcome and links to the authoritative record.
