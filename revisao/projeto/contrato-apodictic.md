## Controlling idea (central claim, approved by the author)

> ICH E6(R3) and ISO 14155:2026 give the investigational site a larger share of the responsibility for quality, but most of the instruments reviewed here were built for the sponsor. This thesis derives a site-level quality framework that complements the sponsor's system, so that a site can act on the risks the sponsor cannot see in time and can show how it controlled its own conduct.

## Anti-idea — what the thesis explicitly does not argue

The lenses must not criticize the thesis for failing to prove any of these. The thesis itself declares them:
1. That the framework has been implemented or that it improves conduct. It is a design study; DSRM demonstration and evaluation were not done (Sections 2.6, 5.7).
2. That the regulatory texts are defective. The gaps follow from the division of responsibility the texts express (Section 3.2).
3. That a site-owned framework is the only workable route (Section 5.1).
4. That the registry sample represents European sites or the global population (Section 2.4.3).
5. That sponsors' quality systems are insufficient. The framework is complementary.

A lens may still flag a passage where the text **overclaims** beyond these limits. That is a proportionality finding, not a demand for evidence the thesis never promised.
