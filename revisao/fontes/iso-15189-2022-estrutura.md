# ISO 15189:2022: estrutura e prefácio (para referência)

Fonte: amostra pública da EN ISO 15189:2022 (SIST EN ISO 15189:2023, iTeh), que reproduz o texto da ISO 15189:2022 sem modificação. Só o sumário, o prefácio, a introdução e o início da norma estão na amostra. O texto completo das cláusulas **não** foi lido.

- Título: *Medical laboratories — Requirements for quality and competence*. 4.ª edição, dezembro de 2022. ISO/TC 212 com CEN/TC 140.
- Substitui a ISO 15189:2012 e a ISO 22870:2016 (POCT).
- Principais mudanças (prefácio): alinhamento com a ISO/IEC 17025:2017, com os requisitos de gestão no fim; inclusão dos requisitos de POCT; maior ênfase na gestão de risco.
- Introdução: a norma "can effectively be applied to other healthcare services, such as diagnostic imaging, respiratory therapy, physiological sciences, blood banks and transfusion services". Os requisitos de gestão de risco seguem a ISO 22367.

## Cláusulas principais

- **4 General requirements**: 4.1 Impartiality; 4.2 Confidentiality; 4.3 Requirements regarding patients
- **5 Structural and governance requirements**: 5.1 Legal entity; 5.2 Laboratory director; 5.3 Laboratory activities; 5.4 Structure and authority; 5.5 Objectives and policies; **5.6 Risk management**
- **6 Resource requirements**: 6.1 General; 6.2 Personnel (competence, authorization, continuing education, records); 6.3 Facilities and environmental conditions; 6.4 Equipment; 6.5 Equipment calibration and metrological traceability; 6.6 Reagents and consumables; 6.7 Service agreements; 6.8 Externally provided products and services
- **7 Process requirements**: 7.1 General; 7.2 Pre-examination processes; 7.3 Examination processes (incl. 7.3.7 Ensuring the validity of examination results); 7.4 Post-examination processes; 7.5 Nonconforming work; 7.6 Control of data and information management; 7.7 Complaints; 7.8 Continuity and emergency preparedness planning
- **8 Management system requirements**: 8.1 General requirements; 8.2 Management system documentation; 8.3 Control of management system documents; 8.4 Control of records; **8.5 Actions to address risks and opportunities for improvement**; 8.6 Improvement; 8.7 Nonconformities and corrective actions; **8.8 Evaluations** (8.8.2 Quality indicators; 8.8.3 Internal audits); **8.9 Management reviews**
- Annex A (normative) POCT; Annex B comparação com a ISO 9001:2015; Annex C comparação com a ISO 15189:2012
