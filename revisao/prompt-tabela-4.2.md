Row 1, Governance
- Nonhospital: The POT, the RACI, the TRE, and the SCR are kept. The POT records the milestone reviews without the MRL, the RACI holds the limit of each holder's authority without the CCDM, and release follows the sponsor's training requirements without the DTRM. - Nonacademic hospital: The POT, the RACI, the TRE, and the SCR are kept, as at the minimum level. Functions are shared among several role holders, with the coordinator as operational integrator.
- Academic: All seven Governance instruments are kept. The MRL records the milestone reviews, the CCDM grades each delegated task, and the DTRM sets the training that each role requires.

Row 2, Risk register and complication surveillance
- Nonhospital: The SRR and the SART are kept. The SRR is opened before the first participant and reviewed at the oversight meeting, and the SART cases are reviewed together at the same meeting, without the SCML and the CSST.
- Nonacademic hospital: The SRR, the SCML, the SART, and the CSST are kept, and the CSST signal review runs on a schedule or on triggers.
- Academic: The same four instruments are kept. The SRR is linked to institutional systems where useful, and the CSST signal review runs on a fixed schedule, independent of daily execution.

Row 3, Procedural competence and stability
- Nonhospital: The SCR and the TRE are kept. Surgeon performance is followed through the re-evaluation of the SCR and the substitutions recorded in the ICDL, without the SLCT.
- Nonacademic hospital: The SCR, the TRE, and the SLCT are kept, and the SLCT review frequency follows device novelty, surgeon experience, procedural complexity, and observed signals.
- Academic: The DTRM is added, and the SLCT is reviewed at fixed points, with access to proctoring where relevant.

Row 4, Operational quality review and imaging
- Nonhospital: The IMC, the PSEAR, the ICDL, and the IQL are kept. The scope and cadence of review are written on the IMC without the IMP, and review is done by a trained peer or the PI, never by the person who did the work. Imaging readiness follows the sponsor's charter without the SIRR, screening is logged in the sponsor's subject identification log without the SIFSL, and visits are recorded in the CRF without the PFVR.
- Nonacademic hospital: The IMP is added, and review is CtQ-focused and done by someone apart from daily execution where possible. Imaging readiness, screening, and visits are handled as at the minimum level.
- Academic: All eight Operational Controls instruments are kept. The SIRR covers imaging qualification and controlled transfer, the SIFSL holds the screening log, and the PFVR holds each visit, with review independent of daily execution.

- Nonhospital: The FUCR and the PIDLR are kept. Follow-up is reviewed in the FUCR from the visit records of the CRF without the PIR, corrective actions follow the process that the site already uses or that the sponsor requests and are followed in the POT without the CAPA log, and lessons are drawn at close-out in the PIDLR.
- Nonacademic hospital: The PIR, the FUCR, the CAPA log, and the PIDLR are kept, and the PIR is read every period for cause and recurrence.
- Academic: The same four instruments are kept, and the PIR may feed institutional quality dashboards.
