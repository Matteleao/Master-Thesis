A
Purpose and use

The SIFSL lists every person considered for the investigation under a code, whatever happened next, and keeps their current status and final outcome. It links each eligibility decision to its Participant Screening and Eligibility Assessment Record (PSEAR) and each enrollment to its record, so that at every cut-off the site can account for every candidate (E). It does not decide eligibility, which belongs to the PSEAR, and it does not calculate rates, which belong to the Performance Indicator Review (PIR).

B
Rules for the log
Codes onlyNo names, initials, birth dates, contact details or clinical details. The key linking codes to identity is kept separately under controlled access.
One line per personUpdate the same line when the status changes; never open a second line for the same person.
Cite, do not copyWrite the PSEAR ID and the enrollment record; the reasons and evidence stay in those records.
No PSEAR before screeningA person only considered does not need a PSEAR until formal screening starts.
Continuation sheetsReprint the register page with the next sheet number; lines are numbered across sheets.
