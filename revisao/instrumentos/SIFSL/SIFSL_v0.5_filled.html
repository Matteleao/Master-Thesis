A
Purpose and use

The SIFSL lists every person considered for the investigation under a code, whatever happened next, with their current status and final outcome. It links each eligibility decision to its Participant Screening and Eligibility Assessment Record (PSEAR) and each enrollment to its record, so that at every cut-off the site can account for every candidate (D). It does not decide eligibility, which belongs to the PSEAR, and it does not calculate rates, which belong to the Performance Indicator Review (PIR).
