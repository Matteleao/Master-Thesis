A
Purpose and Use of This Checklist

Purpose and Use: Use one IMC for one defined site review cycle, covering the applicable operational-control domains for the stated period or trigger. The IMC verifies whether planned controls were executed, whether evidence is reconstructable, and whether the control or response is effective. It does not replace participant-level source review, the Site Risk Register, PI oversight, specialised process records, safety reporting, deviation records or CAPA.

For a focused review, mark domains outside the approved scope as In scope: No and do not reassess them unnecessarily. Every negative or uncertain classification must have a limitation, finding, action, escalation or linked record.

B
Monitoring Classification Guide

Use this guide before completing the domain review. The three dimensions are assessed separately: Execution asks whether the planned monitoring activity occurred; Evidence asks whether the result can be verified; and Effectiveness / Response asks whether the control worked and whether relevant signals were addressed.

Assess whether the monitoring activity planned for the review period was actually carried out. This classification concerns completion of the control activity, not whether the result was satisfactory.

Execution
ClassificationGeneral definitionUse when
CompleteThe monitoring activities applicable to the domain were performed during the review period.All planned activities for the period were completed, even if they identified a signal requiring action.
PartialSome relevant monitoring activities were performed, but one or more material elements were incomplete.Part of the planned review occurred, or the review covered only a limited subset without an approved scope limitation.
Not performedThe planned monitoring activity was not completed and no adequate recovery is documented.The activity was due but did not occur, or its completion cannot be confirmed.
Not applicableThe activity was not applicable to the stated study phase, review period, or selected scope.A documented scope decision explains why no activity was expected.

Classification Guide
B
Monitoring Classification Guide — continued

Assess whether the available records are sufficient and traceable enough to confirm what was done and what was found. Evidence quality is assessed separately from execution and effectiveness.

Evidence
ClassificationGeneral definitionUse when
AdequateEvidence is sufficient, traceable, and allows the reviewer to confirm execution and outcome.The linked controlled record or combination of records is available and internally consistent.
PartialEvidence exists but is incomplete, dispersed, delayed, or does not confirm every relevant element.The reviewer can confirm part of the activity but needs clarification or additional records.
Insufficient / unavailableEvidence cannot confirm that the activity occurred or what result it produced.Records are missing, contradictory, illegible, or not retrievable within the review.
Not applicableNo specific evidence was expected for the documented scope.Use only when the corresponding execution item is also not applicable.
Effectiveness / Response
ClassificationGeneral definitionRequired response
EffectiveThe control is functioning as intended and no unresolved relevant signal remains.No action is required unless another dimension or linked review requires it.
ObservationA minor issue or improvement point exists without current evidence of material control failure.Record the observation; add an action if follow-up is needed.
Not effectiveA recurrence, trend, significant gap, ineffective response, or unresolved relevant signal was identified.Signal, action, owner, due date, and escalation or linked record are mandatory.
Not assessableEffectiveness cannot be determined from the available information.Document the limitation and create an action to complete the assessment.
Review result logic
Execution / Evidence / EffectivenessInterpretationMinimum response
Complete / Adequate / EffectiveControl activity occurred, evidence is reconstructable and no unresolved relevant signal remains.Record the result; no additional action unless linked review requires it.
Complete / Partial / Not assessableActivity occurred, but available records do not support a complete effectiveness judgement.Record the limitation and open an evidence or follow-up action.
Partial or Not performedRequired monitoring was incomplete or absent, regardless of whether isolated evidence exists.Document recovery, owner, due date and escalation route.
Not effectiveA recurrence, material gap, ineffective response or unresolved signal was identified.Link the finding to action, escalation, risk, CAPA or another controlled record.
