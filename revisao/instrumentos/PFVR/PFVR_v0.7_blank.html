Purpose and use

The PFVR records one participant's one follow-up visit: when it happened (B), each component the CIP requires for that visit with its date, completion and timing (C), whether the images and tests are usable (D), what the participant reports (E), the clinical assessment (F) and who did it (G). Problems that need a response go to H, and the visit is classified in I from what C to H show.

A component done on a later contact for the same visit stays in this record, with its own date; a different visit of the CIP gets its own PFVR. Write the short finding that matters for the visit and cite the clinical source, the Image Quality Log (IQL) or the SART for the rest.
