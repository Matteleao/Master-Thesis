A
Purpose and use

The SART is the site's record of one safety case, from the moment the site becomes aware of it until the site's part of the case is closed. It shows that the site recognized the information, protected the person, made its own assessment, reported through the sponsor's route within the CIP window, followed the case and linked it to its other records.

Open the SART at awareness, without waiting for a complete classification. Sections B to D are completed in the first days; Section E grows with each new piece of information; Sections F and G close the site's part.

Boundary. This is a controlled site coordination record. It does not replace source records, the sponsor safety form, the eCRF, the sponsor safety portal, the device-accountability record, the deviation record, the SCML, the CSST, the CAPA log, a regulatory submission or the sponsor/CEC safety determination. It records the site's part of the case and the references to those records.
Unit of analysisOne safety case at the site: one adverse event, one observed device deficiency or one piece of new safety information.
Decision supportedWhether the site recognized, protected, assessed, reported, followed and closed its part of the case in a controlled way.
InputsSource records, participant or user contact, device and accountability records, procedure or visit records (ICDL, PFVR, IQL), sponsor communications, the current CIP and risk information.
OutputsThe local trail of the case; references to the sponsor system; the reporting data read by the PIR (safety reporting timeliness); the links to the SCML, the deviation record and, when triggered, the CSST, POT, SRR or CAPA.

Rule: if an adverse event is associated with an observed device deficiency, apply both the adverse-event pathway and the device-deficiency pathway (Section C).
