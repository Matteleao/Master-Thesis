A
Purpose and use

The SRR is the site's own record of the risks that its execution of the investigation creates: where local staffing, workflow, equipment or experience can compromise a condition that is critical to quality (CtQ) for this investigation. It is opened before the first participant is included and kept current until close-out. It complements the sponsor's risk assessment and the manufacturer's device risk file; it does not copy them.

Pages 1 to 3 are reference: categories, CtQ domains, rating rules and decision rules. The register is G and H: one row per risk, with the same Risk ID on both pages; reprint the pair when it fills. Every review of a risk is entered in I. An urgent local risk is captured in J and then entered in the register. K closes each review of the register.

Risk categories
The category says where in local operations the risk sits. It is not the CtQ domain: one staffing risk can threaten follow-up, safety and data at once.
CategoryTypical local vulnerabilityWhat may be affected
Safety / clinical eventDelayed safety awareness, incomplete assessment or missed reporting.Participant protection and timely clinical decisions.
Procedure / surgeon performanceLearning-curve drift, unplanned substitution or inconsistent technique.Procedural consistency and interpretation of outcomes.
Device accountabilityMixed or poorly segregated stock, missing identifiers or reconciliation gaps.Correct component selection and participant-device traceability.
Data / endpoint reliabilityMissing assessment, unresolved critical query or source-to-CRF mismatch.Complete, reliable and interpretable study data.

Common review triggers: repeated protocol deviations; missed assessment or visit window; delayed safety reporting; device discrepancy or wrong selection; learning-curve or performance signal; monitoring or audit finding; recurrent or ineffective action; change in staffing, training or capacity; change in CIP, device or workflow.

CtQ domains and the factor to protect
In the register, give the domain code and then write, in one sentence, the factor this investigation must protect. A domain name is not a factor. Cite where the factor comes from (CIP, sponsor risk information, local workflow); if it is still unclear, write "to be clarified with the sponsor".

D
Rating rules
Rate with the controls already in place: the result is the current risk. A planned action does not lower a score. Mark a dimension N/A when it does not apply and say why in a few words; N/A is not 1. Do not average or multiply.
Field123
Probability (P)Unlikely; isolated vulnerability.Plausible in normal site conduct.Likely, recurrent or already observed.
Safety (S)No expected effect on safety or participant protection.May affect timely clinical review, protection or safety documentation.May cause or hide serious harm, a serious device effect or a major safety concern.
Procedure (Pr)No expected effect on the conduct of the procedure.May affect consistency, documentation or protocol adherence.May affect surgical appropriateness, intraoperative decisions or technical execution.
Device (Dv)No expected traceability or selection concern.Recoverable discrepancy or delayed reconciliation.May compromise identifiers, component selection or the participant-device link.
Data (Da)No expected effect on critical study data.May affect completeness, timing or source-to-CRF consistency.May compromise endpoint interpretation, eligibility evidence or critical data.
Detection (D)Easy: routine checks find it before it has an effect.Moderate: found only by a planned review, reconciliation or second check.Difficult: may stay hidden until endpoint review, inspection or recurrence.
LowModerateHighCritical
Every applicable field is 1.The highest applicable field is 2.Any applicable field is 3.A condition, not a score: an unresolved serious safety threat, a broken participant-device link, unrecoverable endpoint-critical data, or a threshold the study defines as critical.
A reportable event follows its CIP route at every priority; reporting never waits for the SRR. A study rule that sets a higher priority prevails; cite it.

How to write an entry
Illustrations of the reasoning, not entries to copy. Write the vulnerability and its consequence, not the domain name.
Domain and factor to protectLocal vulnerability and possible consequenceHow to judge the current risk
CtQ7 · Follow-up
Factor: participants complete the required follow-up assessments within their windows.		No physiotherapist is available for the assessment on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Participants who can attend only on those days may miss the visit or leave it incomplete.Assess the real flexibility of appointments, other qualified staff and referral options. Do not assume a safety effect or a high score without looking at the study and the controls in place.
CtQ4 · Device management
Factor: the correct study component is selected and stays traceable to the participant.		Study and marketed components with similar labels share storage without clear segregation, so a wrong component can be picked or the traceability link broken.Assess labeling, segregation, access and reconciliation already in place. A dedicated room is not the criterion; how well the current controls work is.
F
Decision rules after a review
Each decision is recorded in I with its evidence; the status in H is then updated.
DecisionWhen it appliesWho decides
Lower the priorityControls are implemented and a review (I) shows they work. Re-rate every field and keep the trigger. A planned or completed action alone is not enough.PI for High or Critical; otherwise the register owner at the oversight review.
Accept the residual riskThe risk remains but no further action is proportionate. Record the reason, keep the trigger active and set the next review.PI, or the role the PI authorizes for Low and Moderate.
Close the riskThe condition no longer applies: the phase ended, the process changed or the vulnerability was removed with evidence. A closed CAPA does not close a risk by itself.PI when the risk concerns safety, traceability, endpoint data or a CAPA.
Escalate to the CAPA logA control failed or the problem recurs, it is systemic, or the action needs several steps and an effectiveness check. Link an existing CAPA when one covers it.PI or the authorized corrective-action route.
Escalate to the PI (POT) or a milestone (MRL)A PI decision is needed: priority, acceptance, restriction, resources or oversight intensity; or the risk bears on a phase gate.PI; urgent matters use the immediate route.
Escalate to the sponsor or CROThe effect may reach beyond the site, or the CIP or a sponsor threshold requires it. A threat can be escalated before any event occurs.PI, within the CIP route and timing.
