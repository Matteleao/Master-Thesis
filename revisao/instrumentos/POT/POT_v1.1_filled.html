A
Purpose and use

The POT is the PI's plan for overseeing the investigation at the site and the record of every oversight review held under that plan. It settles three things before the first participant: which decisions the PI takes personally, which events must reach the PI at once, and how often the PI reviews the site's quality records and with whom. Each review then leaves a written decision on every record read, so the site can show later how the PI exercised oversight.

The POT records decisions and where each action was sent. It does not repeat the content of the records it reads, and it does not follow actions to their end: each action is followed up in the record that receives it.
