Original (marcado)

Reescrita
Phase 2 comes before Phase 3 because delegation and credentialing, together with training, decide who may take the decisions that make up Phase 3. Original (marcado)
A framework that completely ignored the academic centers or a well-resourced hospital would fail to achieve any significance, since those environments are the most prevalent in shoulder device investigations. The scalability model is therefore a condition of the relevance of the framework, and it answers DR12. Its principle is that the obligations are uniform but the implementation depth is not. Reescrita
Academic centers and well-resourced hospitals are the most common settings for shoulder device investigations, so a framework that left them out would have little relevance. 12.p. 77 · 4.7.2, 1.º parágrafo (inteiro)mudança além da forma
Original (marcado)
Reduced depth raises an obvious objection: a smaller center implementing less may become more exposed at inspection. This concern cannot be disregarded. MDR Article 72(5) permits inspection at an appropriate level, and ISO 14155:2026 Section 9.1(b) requires the sponsor to maintain records demonstrating that the investigation is conducted in compliance with the CIP, the standard, and applicable requirements. A smaller center is protected only to the extent that the minimum clause-linked controls and records remain complete, consistent, current, and retrievable. Scaling may reduce organizational depth, but it cannot remove evidence required to demonstrate compliant conduct.
Reescrita
A smaller center that implements less may be more exposed at inspection, and this objection to reduced depth is a serious one. Under MDR Article 72(5), Member States inspect investigation sites at an appropriate level, and ISO 14155:2026 Clause 9.1(b) requires the sponsor to keep records showing that the investigation complies with the CIP, the standard, and the applicable requirements. A smaller center is therefore protected only as long as its minimum clause-linked controls and records stay complete and consistent, and also current and retrievable. Scaling can reduce organizational depth, but it cannot remove the evidence needed to show that the investigation was conducted in compliance.
Correção de fato: o texto dizia “MDR Article 72(5) permits inspection”. Conferi no MDR, e o artigo diz “Member States shall inspect, at an appropriate level, investigation site(s)”: é uma obrigação dos Estados-Membros, não uma permissão. Isso deixa o argumento do parágrafo mais forte. Section 9.1(b) → Clause 9.1(b) (R23). Saíram os dois-pontos do meio e a frase curta “This concern cannot be disregarded.”, que só dava ênfase.
