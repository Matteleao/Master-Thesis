In pillar sections 4.2-4.5 do NOT refer to the framework's specific instruments: no acronyms (SRR, SLCT, CAPA-as-instrument...), no pointers to Section 4.8 / Table 4.4 / Appendices, no describing an instrument's fields or mechanics, no "the log/the template" pointing at a specific instrument. Using terms like "risk register", "delegation log", "complication log", "learning curve log" as ordinary concepts or practices ("a site needs a register of its risks", "a key governance practice is a delegation log") is fine and wanted: the text must be able to say what is needed to solve a problem.

**Why:** the user asked several times to remove instrument references, then, after I stripped every occurrence of those words (2026-10-04), said that was absurd: those words were generic concepts, not the not-yet-created instruments. Over-literal removal damaged the argument and wasted their time.
**How to apply:** distinguish "a practice/record type the argument calls for" (keep) from "this framework's instrument as such" (remove). When unsure, keep the conceptual wording and ask. Do not do blanket word-level scrubbing. Edge case still open: 4.4.4 describes what the learning curve log and complication log record; leave unless the user asks. See [[thesis-file-state-2026-10-02]].
