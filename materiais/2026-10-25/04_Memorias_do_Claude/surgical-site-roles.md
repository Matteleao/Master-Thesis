The thesis site is a **surgical (shoulder orthopedic) medical-device** investigation under ISO 14155 — NOT classic pharma. Role set for the org/delegation/accountability diagrams:

**Include (site):** PI; Sub-Investigator (surgeon) + Sub-Inv II; Research Physiotherapist (blinded ROM/PROM, Constant & ASES outcomes); Lead/Senior CRC; CRC II; CRC I/CTA; Surgical/OR Coordinator (intra-op data, device/UDI logistics); Imaging Coordinator (radiograph/CT, implant position); Data Manager + Data Coordinator; Research Nurse Manager; Senior CRN; CRN I/II; Site Quality Officer (direct PI access) + QA Specialist; Regulatory Lead + Documentation Specialist; Site Director (present but OUTSIDE the accountability chain).

**External oversight:** Sponsor, CRA/Monitor, Ethics Committee/IRB, Independent Imaging Core Lab.

**Exclude (pharma-only, irrelevant for device):** pharmacist / IMP management (device accountability replaces it — accumulated in Lead CRC, Cl. 7.9), site biostatistician, PK/lab-sample coordinator.

Key decisions: device accountability accumulates in **Lead CRC**; Data Manager has **no direct PI line** (routes via Lead CRC); Regulatory Lead delegated **primarily via Lead CRC (solid)** with an **optional dashed direct link to PI** (decision D6). Use MDR/ISO 14155 device terminology, and "sponsor-complementary" (never "sponsor-neutral"). See [[viz-design-conventions]].
