A
Purpose and Use - site-defined delegation matrix

Purpose and Use: Use one controlled CCDM for a defined site, study and revision. The site identifies its tasks and decisions, assigns criticality and supervision, and records qualifications, permitted boundaries and escalation triggers for approval. The approved matrix defines the task requirements applied in training requirements, person-task assignment and release records. It supports, but does not replace, PI oversight, risk assessment, individual competency evidence or task release.

D
Supervision levels
LevelNameOperational meaning
S0PI-retainedThe delegated person may support preparation but may not perform the final decision or act independently within the task.
S1Active PI reviewThe person may perform defined steps, but the PI or named specialist reviews specified outputs before or at the defined decision point.
S2Defined supervisionThe person may perform the task after release, with a named supervisor, stated restrictions and case-level escalation triggers.
S3Routine oversightThe person may perform the task independently within current instructions; performance is reviewed periodically through normal quality controls.
E
Matrix review and approval

Review the matrix when protocol, procedures, devices, risks, task scope, PI/supervision coverage or relevant quality signals change. The owner assesses affected rows and release records. The PI or accountable approver authorizes the matrix and any restrictions before its effective date.
