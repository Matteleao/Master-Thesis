## Author decisions on the DTRM review (2026-10-06)
- R4: fixed through the shoulder example rewrite.
- R5: training domains 3 (operative and perioperative documentation) and 4 (assessment) are met through ICDL and PFVR; checked in those reviews. No domain column in DTRM.
- R8: ignore.
- C-R4: DTRM cites TRE and RACI as references and states that it feeds them.
- X1: RACI comes last, after TRE.

## Requirement codes (author, 2026-10-08)
DTRM is complete at approval and never waits for TRE: codes describe the training standard only. TR Trained, AS Assessed (method in Section D), CD Conditional (condition states the level), AW Awareness, NA Not applicable; letters chosen so they cannot be read as RACI letters. No text in DTRM may tell the user to check or link individual TRE records (Sections B, F, I cleaned). Section D links each module to CCDM task IDs and gives the assessment method and renewal trigger (Table 4.4 "assessment method ... renewal interval"). Blank form leaves profile and module IDs empty.
