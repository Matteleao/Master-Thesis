## Allocation agreed with the author (2026-10-06)

By design the delegation function is split: CCDM = criticality and supervision per task; DTRM = training each role or task requires; TRE = whether a person is released; RACI = who takes each task, using the CCDM codes. Build order confirmed by the author (2026-10-06, final): CCDM -> DTRM (device- and study-specific training per role/task) -> TRE (individual release against the DTRM) -> RACI (assigns released people to tasks, using the CCDM codes).
- RACI review: R2, R10, R11, R19.
- TRE and DTRM reviews: R3, R5, R7 (competence is demonstrated and defined before release there).
- R4: met jointly by RACI, TRE and DTRM; checked across the three.
- R16: SCR, TRE and DTRM.
- R18: SCR.
- Dropped by the author: R12 (the person running the investigation reads the protocol; the CCDM does not instruct how to code) and R20 (too fine-grained).
- The CCDM itself must state the split (Section B names RACI and DTRM/TRE; no "CDTR"). Applied 2026-10-06.
