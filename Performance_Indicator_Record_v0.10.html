A
Purpose and Use

Aggregate oversight record for one study, one site and one review period: six indicators judged against rules fixed beforehand, with the action taken. It does not replace participant, visit, procedure, safety, deviation or source records. Numbers are entered once, in section D.

Rules. Show n/N and give a percentage only when N is 10 or more. Write 0 or “none”, never leave a blank. Cite source records and sections, never participant narratives. Never change a rule in C after the result is known: issue a new version.

Codes. PSEAR screening/eligibility · SIFSL screening log · PFVR follow-up visit · FUCR follow-up review · SART safety report · SCML complication log · SLCT learning-curve log · ICDL intraoperative/device log · IMC internal monitoring · ICQL imaging log · CAPA log · SRR risk register · MRL milestone review · EDC sponsor data. DEV = deviation register (site or sponsor log) with: date occurred and detected, detected by, domain, minor/important, surgeon or assessor code, corrected yes/no.
