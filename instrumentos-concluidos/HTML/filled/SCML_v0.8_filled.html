A
Purpose and use

This log brings together the site’s complications, adverse events (AEs), adverse device effects and device deficiencies (DDs) for review across cases. The Safety and Adverse Effect Reporting Template (SART) provides the individual safety case. The Intraoperative Checklist and Device Log (ICDL) supplies the procedure and device context.

Output: an indexed set of events with a consistent surveillance profile, current source references and linked follow-up. The Complication Signal Surveillance Template (CSST) uses this index to assess patterns; the Principal Investigator (PI) reads its status through the PI Oversight Template (POT).

The SART retains the clinical chronology, causality assessment and reporting details. Record the current classifications and source links here, with enough context to compare cases. Urgent care and safety notification follow the applicable procedure immediately, without waiting for this log or an aggregate review.

GradeClavien-Dindo definitionExample in shoulder arthroplasty
IAny deviation from the normal postoperative course without drugs, surgery or radiological intervention beyond antiemetics, antipyretics, analgesics, diuretics, electrolytes and physiotherapy; wounds opened at the bedside.Superficial wound problem treated with dressings; transient nerve palsy resolving without treatment.
IINeeds drug treatment beyond grade I, blood transfusion or parenteral nutrition.Wound infection treated with antibiotics; thromboembolism treated with anticoagulation.
IIIaNeeds a surgical, endoscopic or radiological intervention not under general anesthesia.Closed reduction of a dislocation under sedation; aspiration of a hematoma.
IIIbNeeds an intervention under general anesthesia.Open reduction or revision for instability; liner exchange; washout for deep infection.
IVaLife-threatening, single organ dysfunction needing intermediate or intensive care.Pulmonary embolism needing intensive care.
IVbLife-threatening, multiorgan dysfunction.Septic shock from a periprosthetic infection.
VDeath of the participant.

The grade follows the treatment the event needed and is set in the SART. Add the suffix d when a disability remains at discharge (for example IIIb-d). A device deficiency without an adverse event takes N/A.
