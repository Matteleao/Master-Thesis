A
Purpose, Decision and Boundary

This log follows the procedural stabilization of one surgeon performing one defined procedure family with one defined device/system. It records context-sensitive signals close to the operative act so that surgeon-dependent variation can be reviewed before it appears as a later safety, deviation or data-quality problem.

Decision produced: Is execution within the defined scope becoming and remaining sufficiently stable for the current case-complexity context, or is intensified review, retraining, proctoring, restriction or formal escalation required?

This is not an entry credentialing record, a device-training record, an adverse-event form, a device-deficiency form or an intraoperative source record. It receives selected, traceable information from those records and documents the learning-curve interpretation and resulting control decision. Use the surgeon code in this log; the PI keeps the identity key separately.

Use this logic only after completing the scheduled review or a triggered assessment. It converts the conclusion into a controlled response. The selected outcome is recorded in the action log and in the overall status below; it is not a second assessment or a substitute for the trigger table.

DispositionMeaningRequired destination or response
Continue as plannedThe available evidence is stable within the approved scope and comparable case-complexity context. No material surgeon-dependent drift is identified.Record next scheduled review and retain source references.
Intensify reviewA pattern is uncertain, early or limited. It does not yet justify a scope restriction but requires closer observation or a shorter review interval.Define additional cases, reviewer and next review date.
Adapt controlsEvidence supports a local intervention such as renewed device training, temporary proctoring, co-surgery, enhanced documentation review or restriction to lower-complexity cases.Update TRE, SCR or CCDM as applicable; open a dated action and reassessment point.
