A
Purpose and use

The IMP is the site's plan for checking its own work during the investigation, alongside the sponsor's monitoring. It is written and approved before the first participant is included. It does not record any review: each review is run and recorded in the Internal Monitoring Checklist (IMC), which cites the IMP version it followed.

For each of the twelve checks, and any check the site adds, the plan fixes in advance what is checked (C), when, by whom, on which records and how many (D), and what counts as done, evidenced and effective (E). It also sets what calls a review early (F) and where each kind of result goes (G). Rules are set before results exist. A change applies only from its effective date onward and is entered in I; reviews already done stay with the version they used.

Plan boundary
What the plan covers and what it rests on. The plan covers controls the site is responsible for; it does not replace sponsor monitoring.
Investigation(s), sites and phases coveredGLENOS-01 at site 014 only; from start-up to close-out.
Documents the plan is built on (ID / version)CIP v4.2; imaging manual v2.0; SRR-014 v3; delegation log rev. 7; source document map v1.2.
Sponsor monitoring: what the sponsor checks and how its findings reach the siteSponsor CRA visit every 8 weeks with 20% source check of endpoint fields. Findings arrive by follow-up letter to the PI and the coordinator.
Known constraints (staff, time, access to records)One coordinator does most data entry. Radiology archive open to the team on weekdays only. PI on leave 27 Oct to 2 Nov 2026.
If a record picked for review is unavailable (rule for every check)Logged in the IMC as unavailable; the next unit in the list is taken. If more than 1 in 5 picked units are unavailable, the check is rated not assessable.
Outside this planSponsor central image reading; sponsor vigilance and literature screening (until check 11 is included); hospital sterile services audits.
