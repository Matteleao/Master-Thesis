Purpose and Use

Use one PFVR for one participant and one protocol-defined follow-up visit. Record each required component, its method, actual date, evidence and findings. The visit date and component dates may differ.

Record later contacts for this same visit in C and update the linked component in D without obscuring earlier entries. Assess completion after reviewing the component evidence; documentation alone does not correct a missed method or window requirement.

This individual source record feeds the FUCR. Reference clinical, safety and deviation records when needed; aggregate compliance analysis remains in the FUCR.
