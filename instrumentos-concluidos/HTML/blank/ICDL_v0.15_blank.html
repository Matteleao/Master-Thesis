A
Purpose and use

The ICDL is filled in during one participant's procedure, not afterwards, because what happens in the operating room cannot be rebuilt reliably later. It records the release to start (B), the critical steps (C), the times and the case data the surgeon's learning curve record needs (D), every component used with its identifiers (E), changes to the plan (F), complications and device problems (G), the review right after surgery (H) and whether the case can be closed (I). Open actions go to J.

It does not replace the operative note, the device accountability record, safety reporting or the formal deviation record; it captures what is first known here and points to them.
