A
Purpose, Use and decision boundary

Purpose and use: Map current sponsor and study training requirements to site functions. Use this matrix to define what each profile needs, not to create technical training content or authorize staff. Individual completion, competence and release remain in the TRE; task boundaries and supervision remain in the CCDM; the assignment of released people to tasks is recorded in the RACI.

B
Requirement code and consequence
TR - Trained

Complete the module before performing the relevant activity. Evidence of completion is enough.

AS - Assessed

Complete the module and demonstrate competence by the method in D before performing the activity independently.

CD - Conditional

Applies only to the stated activity or trigger. The condition column gives the level that then applies (TR or AS).

AW - Awareness

Know the topic and the escalation route; no technical activity is assigned.

NA - Not applicable

Outside this profile's function; the module does not apply.

Approval of this matrix sets the training standard only. It does not release any person and does not change CCDM task boundaries or supervision.
