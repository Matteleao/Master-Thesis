A
Purpose and use

Read with the CCDM, the RACI is the site's delegation log: the CCDM sets how critical each task is and how much supervision it needs, and the RACI names the function and the person who take it. A C2 or C3 task goes only to a person with a current TRE release for that task.

Approve it at start-up, before the first participant. Afterwards, record changes of holder or backup in Section I and revise the matrix when the CCDM or the DTRM changes.
