A
Purpose and use

The IMC records one internal review of the site's own controls, run as the Internal Monitoring Plan (IMP) sets out. For each check in scope the reviewer rates three things separately: whether the control was done (execution), whether the records show it (evidence) and whether it worked (effectiveness). The IMC cites the IMP version it followed; it does not change the plan's rules after seeing the results.

It refers to records by ID and does not copy their content. It does not judge a single participant, image or event, which stay in their own records, and it does not replace sponsor monitoring, safety reporting or CAPA.

Classification guide
Rate the three dimensions separately for each check in E. A negative or uncertain rating always needs a finding, limitation or action.
RatingUse whenMinimum response
Execution
CompleteAll planned activity for the period was done, even if it found a problem.Rate evidence and effectiveness.
PartialPart of the planned activity was done, or a smaller sample than the IMP without approval.Record what is missing; action with owner and date.
Not performedThe activity was due and not done, or cannot be confirmed.Action with owner and date; consider escalation.
Not applicableThe check does not apply to this phase or scope.Reason in D.
Evidence
AdequateRecords confirm what was done and what was found.None.
PartialRecords confirm only part of it, or are scattered or late.Name the missing record; evidence action.
InsufficientRecords missing, contradictory or not retrievable.Action; effectiveness is not assessable.
Not applicableOnly when execution is also not applicable.Reason in D.
Effectiveness
EffectiveThe control works and no relevant signal is open.None.
ObservationMinor, isolated point with no sign of control failure.Record; action only if follow-up is needed.
Not effectiveRecurrence, material gap, failed response or open signal.Finding, action, owner, date and route.
Not assessableCannot be judged from the records available.State the limitation; action to complete it.
