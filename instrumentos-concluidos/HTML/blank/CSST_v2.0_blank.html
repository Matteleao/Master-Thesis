A
Purpose and use

The CSST is the site's review of its complications taken together. A single event is assessed in the SART and indexed in the SCML; the CSST looks at all of them at once, on the site's own schedule and after a trigger, to see whether events that each seemed explicable share a cause: the same component, the same surgeon, the same moment of follow-up or the same kind of case. A concentration of events is a question to test, not a conclusion about a device or a surgeon.
