A
Purpose and use

The PSEAR records one coded participant's eligibility decision before enrollment: consent in place (B), the screening done (C), the findings that matter for eligibility (D to F), each criterion of the current CIP checked against its evidence (G), open questions and exceptions (H), and the decision (I). No participant is eligible while an item in H is open. Enrollment and handoff follow in J.

Write only what changes, explains or qualifies the decision, and cite the source for the rest. Consent forms, clinical notes, image reports and the CRF stay in their own records; the screening log (SIFSL) keeps every candidate and links to this record.
