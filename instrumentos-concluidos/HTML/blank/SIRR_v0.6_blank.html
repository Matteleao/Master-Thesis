A
Purpose and use

The SIRR records whether the site can carry out the imaging the sponsor's charter requires before the first participant is imaged. Each requirement of the charter is mapped to the local equipment, workflow and people that will deliver it (D to G), gaps get an action (H), and the site concludes whether it can submit its readiness to the sponsor or CRO (I). The charter stays under sponsor control: a requirement the site cannot meet is recorded as a gap or a question, never rewritten.

The SIRR is about the local arrangement, not about any single image set, which is judged in the Image Quality Log (IQL). Signatures attest the site's self-assessment; they do not activate the site, which stays a sponsor decision referenced in K.
