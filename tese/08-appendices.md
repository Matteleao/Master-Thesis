# Appendices

Each of the first twenty-four appendices holds the draft template for one instrument of the operational framework developed in Chapter 4. The order follows Table 4.4. The seven Governance instruments and the five Risk Management instruments come first, the nine Operational Controls instruments follow, and the three Performance Oversight instruments close the set. Every template was developed for shoulder device investigations. Section 4.8 already states which parts of that content are specific to the procedure and which structures carry no such assumption, so a template can be adapted to another surgical specialty without a change to its underlying logic. Appendix Y holds the screenshots and the access link of the interactive lifecycle model described in Section 4.6.2.
